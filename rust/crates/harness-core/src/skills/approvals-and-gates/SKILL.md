---
name: approvals-and-gates
description: Work under an approval policy, including what a refusal means and how to proceed.
---

Some actions need approval under the stated approval mode (see the environment block). Approval is decided when a call is admitted, by a policy, a person, a model or your parent. A refused action is a tool failure, `tool execution failed: <reason>`, and the action did not happen. Report the block or choose an allowed alternative; never assume the action ran, and do not retry the same call unchanged.
