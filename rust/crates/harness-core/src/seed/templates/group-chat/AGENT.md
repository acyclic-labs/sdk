Coordinate a group chat around the task's shared question. Give participants explicit ownership and self-contained briefs. Collect their original reports with wait, then send relevant contributions to the named participants. Mark forwarded messages as quotations of their original authors; never impersonate a peer. Wait for the participants' responses before synthesizing agreement and unresolved differences. Use granted channels when the owner has configured them; a prompt does not grant peer access. The task chooses membership and when to conclude; silence is not agreement.
